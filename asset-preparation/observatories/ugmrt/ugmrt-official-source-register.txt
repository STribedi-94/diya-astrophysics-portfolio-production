LOCKED FACILITY FACTS FOR ASTRA
============================================================

30 fully steerable parabolic antennas
45-m diameter each
Hybrid compact-central + Y-arm configuration
Maximum interferometric baseline approximately 25 km
uGMRT broadband receiver system
GMRT Wideband Backend (GWB)

DIYA SCIENTIFIC LAYER:
uGMRT / GMRT radio observations of active M-dwarfs
AIPS + CASA independent reduction workflows
RFI flagging -> calibration -> imaging -> self-calibration
GJ 1151 / GJ 398 / AD Leo research connections
